## Engine

Babylon.js with TypeScript.

## Camera

First-person camera controlled by mouse look.

## Initial weapon

Hitscan rifle.

## Initial enemy

Simple enemy with detection, pursuit, attack, damage, and death states.

## Input system

Keyboard + mouse for movement and aiming.
Pointer lock for aiming.

## Gameplay Loop

- Player moves and looks around
- Player shoots the enemy
- Enemy detects player and approaches
- Enemy attacks when in range
- Enemy dies when HP <= 0

## Combat Logic

- Weapon has damage, fire rate, range
- Enemy has health, attack range, damage
